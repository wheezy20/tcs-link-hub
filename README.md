# TCS Link Hub

# Lovable Prompt: TCS Linktree-Style QR Hub

## Project: Treasures Christian School - Digital Link Hub
**Purpose**: Create a professional, mobile-first landing page that serves as a central hub for all TCS links. This page will be pointed to by a QR code placed on billboards across Ho, Volta Region. The page must reflect TCS's Christ-centered, holistic educational mission and be easily updateable.

---

## Brand Guidelines (Source: TCS Brand Guidelines v1.0)

### Brand Story & Mission
Treasures Christian School provides Christ-centered, holistic education that integrates academic excellence with character formation and biblical values. The brand promise: "Every child is a treasure."

### Visual Identity

#### Primary Colour Palette
- **Deep Jungle Green**: #005E61 (primary background, primary button)
- **Vivid Lime Green**: #ADF802 (accent, highlights, secondary buttons)
- **Mint Leaf**: #11B87A (logo laurel on light backgrounds)
- **Cayenne Red/Hot Ember**: F15E00 or #E65A00 (accent, secondary accent)

#### Typography
- **Headings**: Cinzel (serif, classical) - Bold weight
  - School name should be prominent and confident
  - Use title case
  - Tracking: -30 (tight letter spacing)
  
- **Body Text**: DM Sans (clean sans-serif)
  - Regular (18px) for body content
  - Medium (24px) for subheadings
  - Tracking: -10
  - Line-height: 130% (1.3 multiplier)

#### Logo & Icon
- Use the TCS crest symbol (treasure chest with laurel wreath and flame)
- Logo specifications: Minimum 80px height
- The crest is circular and should be prominent in the header
- Use white or light color on Deep Jungle Green background
- Never distort, stretch, or modify the logo

#### Design Elements
- **Square Grid Pattern**: Graphic pattern derived from square shapes in logo (can use extended palette colors with sufficient contrast)
- **Layouts**: Open, flexible, generous whitespace
- **Border Radius**: Rounded corners (12-20px) for modern, approachable feel
- **Shadows**: Subtle for depth (0 10px 40px rgba(0,0,0,0.2) or similar)
- **Color Proportion**: Deep Jungle Green 26%, supporting colors distributed

### Tone of Voice (Verbal Identity)
- **Assured & Understated**: Calm confidence, no overselling, timeless rather than trendy
- **Reverent & Grounded**: Respectful, dignified, honors spiritual and academic mission
- **Cultivated & Refined**: Emphasizes character, poise, and leadership development
- **Legacy-Oriented & Relational**: Inclusive language, speaks to parents as stewards, warm but professional

### Key Messaging Pillars
1. **Academic Excellence**: Structured, high-standard learning
2. **Character Formation**: Integrity, responsibility, resilience, Christ-like character
3. **Nurturing Development**: Safe, supportive, attentive environment where every child is known
4. **Family Partnership**: Co-stewards of child development, shared responsibility

### Photography Principles (if images included)
- **Learning in Motion**: Students engaged in meaningful experiences, active discovery
- **Character in Context**: Real interactions demonstrating values in school life
- **Warmth and Authenticity**: Genuine expressions, natural interactions, welcoming without appearing artificial

## Design Specifications

### Layout
- **Mobile-First**: Design for 320px-480px viewport first
- **Desktop**: Optimized for tablets and desktop (responsive scaling)
- **Max Width**: 500-600px for main card on desktop
- **Spacing**: 
  - Container padding: 40px (desktop), 20-30px (mobile)
  - Gap between buttons: 12-16px
  - Top/bottom margin: generous whitespace

### Color Usage by Element
- **Background**: Deep Jungle Green or white
  - If Deep Jungle Green background: use white/light text and Vivid Lime Green accents
  - If white background: use Deep Jungle Green text
  
- **Primary Buttons**: 
  - Option A: Deep Jungle Green background, white or Vivid Lime Green text
  - Option B: Vivid Lime Green background, Deep Jungle Green text
  
- **Secondary Buttons**: Cayenne Red (#F15E00) background, white text

- **Dividers & Accents**: Ocean Field (#2F8C8F) or Vivid Lime Green (#ADF802)

### Typography Hierarchy
1. **School Name (Heading)**: Cinzel Bold, 32-48px, Deep Jungle Green or white
2. **Tagline (Subheading)**: DM Sans Medium, 18-24px, muted color
3. **Button Text (CTA)**: DM Sans Bold or Cinzel Bold, 16-18px, white or high contrast
4. **Body Text**: DM Sans Regular, 14-16px, readable color

### Interactivity
- **Hover States**: Subtle upward movement (2-3px translate), enhanced shadow, slight color shift
- **Active/Pressed States**: Button appears pressed (no lift)
- **Link Targets**: External links open in new tab (target="_blank")
- **Touch Targets**: Minimum 44px height on mobile for accessibility

### Accessibility
- WCAG 2.1 AA compliance
- Strong color contrast (especially on gradient backgrounds)
- Semantic HTML5 (nav, section, footer, etc.)
- Alt text for logo and any images
- Keyboard navigation support (tab, enter)

### Overall Aesthetic
- **Professional yet Warm**: Reflects a Christ-centered educational institution
- **Modern Classicism**: Cinzel brings timeless elegance; DM Sans keeps it contemporary
- **Restrained Confidence**: Bold colors are used purposefully, not excessively
- **Spacious & Safe-Feeling**: Generous whitespace suggests care and intentionality

### Mood & Personality
- Nurturing, structured, dependable, sophisticated, approachable
- Communicates stability and spiritual grounding
- Avoids flashy trends; aims for timeless design

### Pattern & Texture (Optional)
- Subtle square grid pattern using brand colors (especially on backgrounds)
- Use pattern as texture/depth, not as primary focus
- Ensure pattern has sufficient contrast and doesn't interfere with readability
